Physiological Keys

Oxygenated Blood (#E63946)

High concentration of oxygen. Travels from Lungs to Left Heart, then pumped through Systemic Arteries to organs.

Deoxygenated Blood (#4FC3F7)

Depleted oxygen, rich in carbon dioxide. Returns from organs via Systemic Veins to Right Heart, then pumped to Lungs.

Flow Circuits

Pulmonary: Loop between Heart and Lungs. Recharges blood with oxygen.

Systemic: Loop between Heart and Body organs. Delivers oxygen and nutrients to tissues.
